Top 5 Maintenance Tips for Summer

Admin May 6, 2026 Maintenance Tips

Summer is a great time to enjoy the outdoors with your golf cart. However, the heat can take a toll on the vehicle's components. Here are 5 essential maintenance tips to keep your golf cart running smoothly during the hot summer months.

1. Check Your Batteries Often

The heat causes battery water to evaporate faster. Make sure you check the water levels in your batteries at least once a week. Always use distilled water to refill them.

2. Inspect the Tires

Hot asphalt can increase tire pressure, leading to a bumpy ride or even blowouts. Check your tire pressure regularly and ensure they match the manufacturer's recommended levels.

3. Clean the Undercarriage

Dirt and debris can accumulate under your cart, trapping heat and potentially causing the motor to overheat. Give the undercarriage a good wash every few weeks.

"Proper maintenance not only extends the life of your golf cart but ensures a safe and enjoyable ride for everyone." - GolfCartPro Expert

By following these simple steps, you can avoid costly repairs and ensure that your golf cart is always ready for the next adventure.
